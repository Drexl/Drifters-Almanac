# Drifter's Almanac

A free, unofficial Embers Adrift companion for crafting and equipment planning.

Open [Drifter's Almanac](https://drexl.github.io/Drifters-Almanac/) in a modern
browser. No account or installation is required.

## Crafting

Browse recipes and materials, preview crafted stats at your profession levels,
and compare material combinations in Finder. Save Finder favorites in your
browser. Search by name or use filters such as `mat:copper`, `prof:blacksmith`
and `stat:armor`. Settings holds your profession levels.

## Equipment and characters

Loot Browser lists equipment and its known sources. Compare builds in Build
Finder, create characters with several builds, and equip items from Loot Browser,
Recipes or Finder. Saves stay in the browser on that device.

This is a work-in-progress site. Build Finder is unfinished, and the Combat
Simulator is not ready for normal use.

## Downloads

New standalone HTML downloads are no longer published. Existing Crafting
Browser downloads still work, but receive no updates. A desktop app is planned;
it is not available yet.
